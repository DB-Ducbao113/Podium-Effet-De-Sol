# P2.1 — CAD author commercial permission and provenance request (NOT SENT)

This document prepares a formal rights request. No message was sent to any author or repository.

## Intended use to disclose

Podium Effet De Sol is a public F1-education-inspired interactive engineering website that may become commercial. Users interact with rendered model geometry and can view, rotate, isolate and disassemble components in a browser. GLB geometry and material data are distributed to users' browsers by the website, so this is **not merely private internal CAD study**.

Proposed rights need to cover:

1. Obtain and work from the named original STEP/CAD files.
2. Modify, decimate, retessellate, split and/or rebuild geometry for HIGH/BALANCED/PERFORMANCE GLBs and animations.
3. Publicly display, stream, host and distribute the derived GLBs (and associated CAD metadata) through the website/CDN, to users worldwide, including commercial use.
4. Indefinite nonexclusive permission or a compatible clear license, attribution obligations and any mandatory source redistribution.
5. Identification of any incorporated third-party CAD, trademark, wheel/brake maker, sponsor, patent or design rights that the author cannot grant.
6. Whether the author requires visible marks/logos, project name changes, or the release of derivative source CAD.
7. Confirmation that no real F1 team/series affiliation or certified design is implied.

## Draft to send to the rightsholder

Subject: Permission to use racing CAD models in an interactive engineering education website

Hello,

I am developing Podium Effet De Sol, an interactive website explaining formula-style racing engineering. I am reviewing your CAD work at [exact source repository URL / commit / STEP file path].

We would like permission to create our own optimized 3D visualizations from specified CAD files, keeping named components such as the hub, upright, brake rotor, caliper and fasteners. Our website may be commercial. The converted GLB geometry would be downloaded by browsers, so the use is a public distribution of adapted CAD geometry, not only personal research.

Could you confirm in writing whether you own the rights to these specific files and whether you grant permission to modify and publicly display/distribute derived GLB files in a commercial web application? Please also clarify attribution, source-sharing obligations, license duration, and whether any third-party manufacturer models or marks are included.

We will not claim the models are manufacturer-certified F1 hardware. We can credit your design appropriately according to agreed terms.

Thank you.
