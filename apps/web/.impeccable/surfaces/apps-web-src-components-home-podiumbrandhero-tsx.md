## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a live aero dossier: data, engineering and the Model B show car occupy one operational field. It refuses the category-default split hero where copy sits beside a decorative poster.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian and titanium surfaces are cut by crimson longitudinal rails, cool technical light and restrained grid lines. Barlow Condensed carries editorial display roles, Space Grotesk carries navigation and reading, and IBM Plex Mono carries timing and points. Controls use crisp chamfered edges, luminous focus states and instrument-like labels.

STORY: Visitors understand that Podium EDS joins current race intelligence, engineering explanation and an interactive Model B show car. They can immediately enter the 3D showroom or continue into engineering, then see the same visual system applied to standings and results.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 64px instrument rail anchors the top. The headline occupies the left two-fifths at editorial scale; the live aero stage occupies the right three-fifths with visible floor light and depth. A race-data telemetry rail closes the viewport. The primary Showroom action sits directly below the opening statement.
