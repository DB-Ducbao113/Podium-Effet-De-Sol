# Podium EDS design status

The previous IGNITION, V7, V8, V8.2 and V9 visual plans have been retired. No replacement creative direction is approved yet. Existing pages remain the current implementation until a new plan is agreed and built.

## Durable boundaries

- The original Podium EDS logo is the canonical brand asset. Preserve its source artwork and aspect ratio.
- Model B is the current Showroom vehicle. Do not silently reuse it on Home or treat it as an original AERO-01 model.
- EDS-G01 is retired from the active experience. Historical withdrawal and provenance records remain in `docs/asset-manifests/` and `docs/asset-licenses.md`.
- Local review assets in `.local-assets/` and source files in `source-assets/` are outside the public build. Moving an asset into production requires a clear license and provenance record.
- Keep real results and editorial claims tied to their verified sources. Do not present concept visuals or illustrative telemetry as official data.
- Preserve keyboard access, readable content, reduced-motion behavior and a usable fallback for 3D experiences.

Current CSS tokens and page composition are implementation details, not requirements for the next design. Record the new direction here only after it is chosen.
