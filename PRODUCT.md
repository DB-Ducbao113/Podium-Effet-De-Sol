# Podium EDS product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Motorsport readers exploring bilingual race-engineering explanations and an interactive car reference.

## Product Purpose

Podium EDS connects racing results, editorial engineering content, and a 3D vehicle experience. F3 lets readers select seven anatomy topics and relate the explanation to a camera view of Model B.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing routes and data behavior remain intact. Model B is a local DEV-only, Meshopt-compressed reference model with one mesh; its annotations are approximate and cannot support true exploded assemblies. The 2022 reference must not be portrayed as a verified 2026-regulation car. Production, hosting, and release remain on hold pending separate owner approval.

## Brand Commitments

Existing graphite, crimson, titanium design language; Vietnamese and English content; clear attribution to Luis Adrian V. under CC BY 4.0.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing Showroom model loader, adaptive graphics, camera presets, seven approximate hotspots, and original engineering articles. No verified independent geometry for the seven parts.

## Product Principles

- Technical claims remain educational and qualified.
- Interaction never obscures reading or requires animation.
- 3D loading is route-scoped and has an accessible fallback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard controls, visible focus, reduced motion, bilingual explanations, and fallback content when WebGL is unavailable.
